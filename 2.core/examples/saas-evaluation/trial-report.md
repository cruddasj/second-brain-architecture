# Task service trial report

Synthetic teaching example: all services, accounts, prices and observations are invented.

## Trial scope

A fictional operations team tested Task Orchard and Task Meadow from 2026-01-05
to 2026-01-14 with 100 synthetic tasks, 200 comments and five test accounts.

## Observations

| Measure | Task Orchard | Task Meadow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly quote for five seats | 40 currency units | 30 currency units |
| Exported task titles | 100 of 100 | 100 of 100 |
| Exported comments | 200 of 200 | 0 of 200 |
| Median task creation time, 20 attempts | 18 seconds | 15 seconds |

Neither trial included attachments, cancellation, outage recovery or accessibility
assessment. These are fictional observations, not current market prices.
